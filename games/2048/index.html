How to Play 2048

Objective: Slide matching numbered tiles across the 4×4 grid. When two identical numbers collide, they merge into their sum!

Reach the 2048 tile to win. You can keep playing past 2048 to hit 4096 or higher!

Controls:

  • Mobile: Swipe your finger in any direction across the board.
  • Desktop: Use ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D.
  • Undo: Click the Undo button or press U / Ctrl+Z.

How to Play

Use your arrow keys or swipe on mobile to slide the tiles. When two tiles with the identical number touch, they merge into one! Merge your way to the 2048 tile, or undo any mistake with the Undo button.
