Game Rules & Collision Physics

Your serpent begins at a standard length of three segments moving rightward at an accessible starting pace of 150ms per step. Movement is continuous and head-directed; the body follows the precise historical path traversed by the head.

  • Energy Food & Growth: Each food pellet collected awards exactly 10 points and extends the serpent by one trailing segment. Food only spawns on verified unoccupied grid cells.
  • Lethal Wall Boundaries: Crashing head-first into any of the outer perimeter boundaries (X < 0, X ≥ 20, Y < 0, Y ≥ 20) instantly terminates the game. Boundaries do not wrap around.
  • Self-Collision Hazards: Colliding with any part of your own trailing body causes immediate defeat.
  • Vacating Tail Physics: On normal ticks where food is not eaten, the final tail segment vacates its cell simultaneously as the head advances. This allows precision players to follow directly behind their own retreating tail without triggering a false self-collision.

Proven Survival Tactics for High Scores

  1. The Perimeter Highway: Whenever your body length exceeds 15 segments, resist the temptation to cut straight across the central arena. Instead, hug the outer perimeter walls until you are perpendicular to the target food pellet, then dart in and retreat back to the outer ring.
  2. Serpentine Sweeping (The Zig-Zag): When navigating crowded open zones, move in systematic back-and-forth S-curves or zig-zag patterns. This tightly packs your body loops against one wall and keeps the remaining 75% of the board wide open.
  3. Never Box Yourself In: Before turning into a pocket or cul-de-sac between your own coils, verify that an open exit corridor exists with a path wider than your snake's length. Entering a blind pocket with high body mass is the leading cause of late-game death.
  4. Utilize Directional Input Buffering: The input engine incorporates a two-step FIFO queue. You can quickly tap ↑ then → in rapid succession to execute a tight 90-degree corner before the next tick executes, ensuring smooth movement without missed keystrokes.
