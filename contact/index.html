Frequently Asked Questions

Is Browser Arcade completely free?

Yes! There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paywalls. All games are 100% free to play directly in your browser.

How are my high scores saved?

Your best score and preferences are saved locally on your device via standard browser localStorage. No account creation or server storage is required.

Can I play on my mobile phone?

Browser Arcade is designed mobile-first. The 2048 game supports smooth touch swipe gestures and automatically adapts to your screen size.
